STORYFRAME / PRODUCT LAUNCH BRIEF

1. What are you launching? Write one sentence.
2. Who is it for? Name one specific audience.
3. What frustrating moment does it improve?
4. Which real product action proves the benefit?
5. What should the viewer do next?

YOUR THREE MOMENTS
Opening (0-4s): the problem or promise
Proof (4-12s): one action and a readable result
Close (12-16s): product name and one invitation

Before recording: use an authorised account, remove personal data, and keep the original recording. Mark fixture data. Adjust timing to make the product readable.
